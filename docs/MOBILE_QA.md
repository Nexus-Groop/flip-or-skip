# Mobile / Tablet Acceptance Checklist

Do not treat FLIP OR SKIP as launch-ready until the CEO has personally approved this checklist on real hardware.

## Core calculation
- Enter buy and sale values and confirm Profit / ROI / Margin update immediately.
- Add shipping and other costs and confirm outputs change.
- Change marketplaces and confirm estimated fee behavior updates.
- Choose Custom and verify both percentage and fixed fees.
- Move the ROI target slider repeatedly and confirm the target and verdict update without lag.
- Clear the form and confirm all fields and outputs reset.

## Touch and layout
- No horizontal scrolling.
- No clipped text, cards, dropdowns, or buttons.
- Inputs are easy to tap without zooming.
- Slider is comfortable to drag with a finger.
- Keyboard does not cover the field being edited.
- Page remains usable in portrait and landscape.
- Help buttons and Quick Guide are easy to tap and close.
- Light/dark toggle works without layout shift.

## Visual quality
- Black/green FLIP OR SKIP identity feels consistent with desktop.
- Spacing feels intentional rather than compressed desktop styling.
- Verdict and key metrics are visible quickly without excessive scrolling.
- Cost breakdown is readable at phone width.
- Typography is premium and not oversized or childish.

## Devices
At minimum:
- iPhone / Safari
- iPhone / Chrome if available
- Tablet / Safari or Chrome

## Release rule
Any failed core interaction blocks launch. Cosmetic notes can be prioritized, but the CEO must explicitly approve the final mobile experience.

🛒 1. The Deal

The Deal — what goes here?

Buy Price: what you will actually pay for the item.

Expected Sale: what you realistically think the item will sell for. Recent sold prices are better than the highest asking price you see.

Shipping: what you expect to spend sending the item to the buyer. Leave it at $0 if you don't know yet.

Other Costs: extra costs connected to the flip, such as packaging, cleaning, repairs, or replacement parts.

Example: You find a game for $20 and realistically expect to sell it for $50. Enter $20 for Buy Price, $50 for Expected Sale, and your estimated shipping cost.

⚙ 2. Fees & Target

Fees & Target — what do these mean?

Marketplace Fee: the percentage of the sale that the selling platform takes. Use the fee your platform actually charges.

Target ROI: the minimum return you want from the money you put into the item. Higher target = more selective deals.

Example: If you spend $25 and make $10 profit, your ROI is 40%. Setting a 40% target means you want at least that level of return.

How Flip or Skip works

Use this before buying a resale item. Enter what you expect to spend and what you realistically expect to sell it for. Flip or Skip handles the math so you can make a faster decision.

What will the item actually cost you? This is required.

2

3

Add known costs

4

Set your target ROI

BUY clears your target, CONSIDER is close, and SKIP misses it by more.
